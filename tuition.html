What's included

  • All lessons with the class teacher
  • Course book and activity materials
  • A progress note every month
  • A parent–teacher check-in every term

Good to know

  • Brothers and sisters: ask about our family discount.
  • Exam registration fees, if any, are charged separately.
  • Tuition is paid in advance. Missed classes can be made up within the same term.

Questions parents ask

Can we try a class before paying?

Yes. Every new family can join one free trial class. There is no payment and no obligation.

How do I choose between monthly, term and package?

Monthly is the most flexible. Term and package plans cost less per class if you plan to stay for longer.

What if my child misses a class?

You can make up a missed class in another session of the same level within the term, depending on available seats.

How do we find the right level?

Book a free trial. The teacher will chat with your child, then recommend the best level.
